ABOUT RESUMEAI

Building a smarter path from resumes to opportunities.

ResumeAI is an AI-assisted resume screening project designed to help analyse graduate candidate profiles through structured data and transparent scoring.

🎯

Our Objective

The objective of ResumeAI is to simplify the initial candidate screening process by converting resume-related information into meaningful candidate insights.

🤖

How It Works

Candidate attributes such as skills, experience, projects, internships, CGPA and achievements are evaluated using a transparent suitability scoring framework.

ResumeAI aligns with Sustainable Development Goal 8, which focuses on decent work and economic growth. The project aims to support the employment process by making initial candidate analysis faster, more structured and easier to understand.
